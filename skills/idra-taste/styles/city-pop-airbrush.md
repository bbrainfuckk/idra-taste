# City pop airbrush (Japan, late 1970s to 1980s)

**Where it comes from.** Record sleeves and illustration for Japanese city pop and resort culture. Airbrushed skies, swimming pools, palm trees, empty modern buildings and convertibles, painted with clean light and almost no people. Study for principles: Hiroshi Nagai, Eizin Suzuki.

**Medium.** Airbrush and gouache illustration printed in four-colour offset, framed by wide white or coloured borders on a 12-inch sleeve.

## Palette
Clear, sunlit, saturated but never neon.

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Zenith blue | `#1f5fbf` | top of sky |
| Horizon cyan | `#7fd3f0` | lower sky |
| Pool turquoise | `#00aa93` | water |
| Building white | `#f7f4ec` | walls, shading in blue-grey |
| Shadow blue | `#3d5588` | hard cast shadows |
| Coral | `#ff7a6b` | sunset band, towels, cars |
| Palm green | `#2f7d4f` | foliage |

## Type
- Thin or light italic sans for English titles, wide letter spacing, small. **Jost** light, **Josefin Sans** light, or **Didact Gothic** work. The art is the hero, the type is a caption.
- Optional: script signature for the artist name.
- Japanese title in small gothic on the obi strip (the paper band around Japanese LP sleeves), which is a great framing device.

## Composition
- One clear vanishing point, hard horizon, sky taking 50 to 70 percent.
- Geometry of modernist buildings, poolside tiles, diving boards, railings: clean lines and hard shadows.
- Wide border around the picture. Sometimes a square image inside a square sleeve with the title in the margin.

## Texture and print
- Smooth airbrush gradients in the sky are correct here, but they are long linear gradients (zenith to horizon), not radial glows.
- Very fine grain and a hint of halftone rosette in the print.
- Slight warmth shift as if printed in 1983 (lift blacks, warm whites).

## Fake tells
- Vaporwave: Greek statues, Windows 95, glitch, Japanese text as decoration, pink-purple grids. Different decade, different meaning.
- People and faces as the focus. The originals are about empty places.
- Neon glow, night scenes with synth sunsets. City pop art is daylight or dusk, rarely neon.

## Works for
Album and playlist covers, travel and hospitality, summer campaigns, beverage labels, lifestyle social posts, poster series.
