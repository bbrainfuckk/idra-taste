# <Movement name> (<years>)

Use this template when a brief needs a movement that is not in the library. Research it first (period sources, archives, museum collections, the medium used), then write the recipe in the project folder (`.idra-taste/recipes/<name>.md`) before designing. If it proves useful, add it to `styles/`.

**Where it comes from.** Place, years, purpose (what the work was for), and the culture around it. Names to study for principles, never to copy.

**Medium.** How it was physically made: process, number of colours, paper or surface, typical sizes. The medium decides which marks are allowed.

## Palette
Inks or paints with hex values and a role for each (dominant, secondary, accent, key). Note how colours were mixed or overprinted.

## Type
Open fonts that fit (check each name exists), how type was produced at the time (hand-lettered, letterpress, phototype, bitmap), and the setting rules.

## Composition
The structural habits: symmetry or asymmetry, grid, axis, density, scale contrast, framing, what fills the edges.

## Texture and print
Which `print.js` marks apply and at what strength. What the medium never produced.

## Motifs
Recurring images and devices, and what they meant.

## Fake tells
How lazy revivals and generated imitations get it wrong. At least four.

## Works for
Formats and subjects where this direction fits.
