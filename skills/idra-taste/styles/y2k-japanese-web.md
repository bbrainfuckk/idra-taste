# Y2K Japanese personal web (1998 to 2006)

**Where it comes from.** Personal homepages, fan sites and shop sites on Japanese hosting services, made by hand in HTML tables, plus the street fashion culture of Harajuku documented by magazines such as FRUiTS. Tiny, dense, cute, collected.

**Medium.** Screen at 800 x 600. HTML tables, framesets, small GIFs, bitmap fonts, 88 x 31 buttons, blinking and marquee text.

## Palette
Pastel ground with candy accents, or white with saturated tiny elements.

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Pastel pink | `#ffd6e8` | page background |
| Mint | `#c8f5e3` | table cells |
| Lavender | `#e2d6ff` | sidebar |
| Candy red | `#ff3366` | links, hearts |
| Sky | `#66ccff` | headers |
| Black | `#000000` | 1 px borders |

## Type
- Tiny: 10 to 12 px bitmap Japanese (**DotGothic16** at its native 16 px is the largest), **MS Gothic**-like monospace feel. Latin in small pixel fonts.
- Kaomoji and symbols as real typography: (*^_^*), ☆, ♪, ※, ■. Used as people used them, in context.
- Link lists, update logs with dates ("2003.04.12 日記更新"), counters, guestbook links.

## Composition
- Centred fixed-width layout (about 760 px), a top banner image, a left menu of small buttons, a main column of updates.
- Lots of small framed boxes, 1 px dotted borders, tiled backgrounds of tiny patterns.
- Everything small. The page rewards close looking.

## Texture
- Pixel GIF decorations: sparkles, ribbons, animals, dividers, all at 1x with no smoothing.
- Tiled 8 x 8 or 16 x 16 background patterns.
- Optional: low-frame-rate GIF animation (4 to 8 fps).

## Fake tells
- "Y2K" chrome blobs and liquid metal 3D. That is Western 2020s revival.
- Large type and generous whitespace. These pages were dense.
- Kaomoji used randomly, or broken Japanese. Real sites were written by real people.
- Modern rounded cards with shadows.

## Works for
Fan pages, music and fashion microsites, event pages for young audiences, portfolio easter eggs, merch shops with personality, social graphics.
