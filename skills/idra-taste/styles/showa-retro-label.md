# Showa retro label (Japan, 1950s to 1970s)

**Where it comes from.** Matchbox labels, soda and candy wrappers, bathhouse signs, toy boxes and shop noren from postwar Japan. Printed cheaply in 2 or 3 inks, drawn by hand, friendly and slightly off.

**Medium.** Letterpress or cheap offset in 2 to 3 spot inks on coloured or cream paper. Registration is loose. Ink sits heavy at edges.

## Palette
Two or three inks plus paper. Choose one set:

| Set | Inks |
|---|---|
| Soda | Bright Red `#f15060`, Blue `#0078bf`, cream paper `#f4ead2` |
| Candy | Pumpkin `#ff6f4c`, Teal `#00838a`, Yellow `#ffe800` |
| Bathhouse | Indigo `#484d7a`, Brick `#a75154`, natural paper `#efe6d2` |

Overprints produce the extra colours. Paper shows through as the "white".

## Type
- Rounded and friendly Japanese: **Zen Maru Gothic**, **Kaisei Decol**, **Mochiy Pop One**, **Potta One**, **Yusei Magic** (all OFL, Google Fonts).
- Hand-drawn lettering is better: thick strokes, rounded ends, slight wobble (`IdraPrint` rough filter at low scale).
- Horizontal Japanese in older signs ran right to left. Use it only where it is true to the object (shop signs, labels), and only with real words.
- Latin, if any, is naive: wide, slab, or hand-inked.

## Composition
- Centred badge, circle or shield, with a mascot or product drawing.
- Star bursts with price or claim. Ribbons. Scalloped or zig-zag borders.
- Small text around the edge (maker's name, address, contents).

## Texture and print
- Clear misregistration (2 to 4 px at screen size) between the inks.
- Ink squash: slight dark edge on solids, uneven fill (`IdraPrint` ink filter at medium strength).
- Aged paper: a flat warm tone, not stains and scratches.

## Fake tells
- Grunge brushes, coffee stains, torn edges on everything. Age shows as tone, not damage.
- Too many colours. The cost of each ink was real.
- Perfect circles and vector-clean stars. Hand cut and hand drawn.

## Works for
Packaging, labels, café and food brands, merch, stickers, kids' products, menus, friendly campaigns.
